# Super Robot Wars F — real-route and shared scenario facts

Plot facts only, paraphrased from the real-route walkthrough at `srworld.net` (`real/1-5.htm` through `real/31-34.htm`) and the branch chart at `gamenandemo.stweb.jp` (`shinario1.htm`). Strategy, money, and HP tables are omitted. Nothing below is taken from F Final.

## Source limits

- The walkthrough mixes plot with tactics and with the author’s guesses. Guesswork is marked. It does not reveal motives the scene itself leaves hanging.
- Five shared stages have a full story in the walkthrough body but **no Japanese title in the fetched text** (likely an image heading). Their titles below come from the branch chart, matched by order. Those stages are: 南海の死闘, 浮上, ドクーガの野望, 大将軍ガルーダの悲劇, オペレーション・レコンキスタ.
- The walkthrough spells some titles differently from the chart. Chart forms are used as the headings. Walkthrough forms are noted once.
- Stages 14B, 15B, and 16B are short “same as another stage” notes. They are marked thin.
- The chart lists super-only stages that these pages do not narrate. Those plots are not filled in.
- The guide’s own spellings wobble: コーウェン / ゴーウェン, リツコ / リッコ, ワサン / ワザン, ハッシャ / ハツシャ, ベンケラー (stage 2) vs ヘッケラー (stage 11 on). Both spellings are kept. They are not merged into one person unless the scene says so.
- Angel labels are the guide’s: Sachiel is the 3rd Angel, Shamshel the 4th. Both appear more than once.

Real-only opening, then a shared trunk, then branches:

1. 立ちこめる暗雲 → ザ・ストレンジャー → シャングリラ・チルドレン → 非情のテロリスト → ヒイロとリリーナ → 使徒、出現

4. Shared trunk: 南海の死闘 → 裏切り → 使徒、再び → 大ピンチ、コンバトラーＶ合体不可能 → 浮上
5. After 浮上, pick a ship:
   - **Goraon first** (Amazon, South America): ゴラオン救出 → 標的はロンド=ベル → 驚異のデビルガンダム
   - **Gran Garan first, real route** (near Kiev): デビルガンダム出現 → ロンド=ベルへの刺客 → ゴラオンの危機
   - The chart’s super Gran Garan line (グランガラン救出, then デビルガンダム再び) is not narrated here.
6. Detour, then ネルフ襲撃 → ドクーガの野望 → ジャブロー侵入（前）（後） → 目覚めよ、超獣機神 → ミーアの決意 → 大将軍ガルーダの悲劇 → その名は東方不敗 → DCの陰謀 → アデレード奪回（前）（後） → オペレーション・レコンキスタ → コロニーの反逆 → ロンド=ベル宇宙へ → 和平成立 → 再会のサンクキングダム → F Final.

## 1. 立ちこめる暗雲

Walkthrough heading: 立ちこめろ暗云. Real route.

- The protagonist arrives at the independent unit Londo Bell as an intern recommended by Lieutenant General Cowen (コーウェン), and greets Amuro and the other veterans.
- Londo Bell already fights with civilian helpers. Rank is loose. The protagonist is told to let Amuro and the captain use their name.
- Luna 2 is under attack by DC remnants. On the way they discuss the Titans: Jamitov’s faction nearly controls the Federation, taxes space residents hard, and drives some colonies toward the DC remnants. The guide says Titans was built around part of those remnants so Jamitov could also pressure Londo Bell.
- At Luna 2 an internal explosion is a feint. Captain Bright sends Lieutenant Emma and Keith inside. The Black Tri-Stars (Gaia, Mash, Ortega) in Dom IIs, with Zakus, appear outside.
- Turn 2: Emma and Keith return. On the enemy phase of turn 5, Cima reinforces from the bottom in a Gerbera Tetra. On the next ally phase, Getter 1 appears at the upper left.
- After the fight, Ryoma’s team explains they were going to Side 7 to see Dr. Kasshu, a colleague of Dr. Saotome in biological engineering, who is missing. They travel with Londo Bell for now. Kouji is in North America helping the Tesla Reich Institute. Nobody knows why Kasshu vanished, or what was being developed in secret.

## 2. ザ・ストレンジャー

Walkthrough heading: ザ˙ストレンジャー. Real route.

- Side 7 refuses a meeting with Kasshu: a recent military order, details unknown. Dr. Saotome tells Ryoma’s team to stay with Londo Bell. Getter is officially enrolled.
- Next stop is resupply at Londenion. A non-Earth-language emergency signal comes in. Bright orders a combat alert.
- Daba and two companions are cornered and choose to fight. The attackers are Heavy Metals of the Pentagona visitors, the Poseidal military. The treaty with the Federation is top secret. Poseidal’s leader Nei, in the Auge, declares war: she is only following orders.
- Daba’s group fights alongside Londo Bell. The main force arrives on turn 3. Nei retreats on turn 12.
- Afterward: the three are a tiny anti-Poseidal resistance; most of that army is still on Pentagona. Amuro thinks the spirit Lilith looks like Cham. Lilith wants to meet such a being. A recording of Poseidal’s declaration says the Federation imprisoned and executed their ambassador. Bright says that negotiation was top secret. Daba says Poseidal seized Pentagona and was given an unknown group’s space-navigation method. Bright will not enlist them on his own authority and contacts Cowen. They reach Londenion and finish resupply.

## 3. シャングリラ・チルドレン

Walkthrough heading: シャングリラ˙チルドレン. Real route.

- Chris (クリス) thinks the Pentagonans seem like Earth people. Amu (アム) says a translator is why. Kyao (キャオ) helps service machines.
- Bright cannot reach the General Staff. Amuro recalls a similar blackout during the Inspector invasion four months ago. Daba cannot say whether Poseidal’s helper is the Inspectors.
- Shangri-La’s garrison calls: suspected Poseidal units are inside. Heavy Metals with beam coating crush the defending mobile suits. Gablae, one of the Thirteen, boasts; Chai warns that reinforcements may come. Gablae moves on the colony’s military facilities.
- This is Judau’s home. He sends his sister Leina to shelter. Elle’s group launches old Zakus. Judau sorties in a Nemo. Londo Bell arrives. Daba recognizes Gablae and calls him a tool of Poseidal’s dictatorship. Gablae calls Daba a traitor.
- Turn 3: the main force arrives. If either Gablae or Chai is shot down, Poseidal retreats. After that retreat, Wing Gundam (Heero) appears on the right as a third party, ignores hails, and retreats below half HP. The guide states that shooting him down does **not** change later plot.
- Afterward Leina insists on joining: Judau fights to protect her, but many others are trapped by war. Beecha, Elle, and the others volunteer; Amuro agrees and calls them Newtype material. Judau wanted Leina to stay on the colony, but Londo Bell seems safer. The Shangri-La group joins.

## 4. 非情のテロリスト

Real route.

- Still unable to reach the General Staff, they descend toward Federation HQ at Jaburo.
- A personality-dependent kitchen scene with the protagonist is mentioned and not quoted. Judau worries about the colony. Amuro says the aliens are unlikely to hit an unarmed Shangri-La again; the problem is Earth.
- Entering the atmosphere, Wing Gundam returns. Heero says the target is correct this time. Mashymre’s DC unit also arrives, devoted to Haman. He mistakes Wing for a Londo Bell machine, then for a sympathizer. Heero says he has nothing to do with DC and that only Londo Bell is the target. Heero stays a third party. No special retreat event is listed.
- Afterward they are surprised DC still has time to attack during the Poseidal invasion. They enter the atmosphere for Jaburo. Heero already knows that destination and continues the mission. An air-defense satellite, previously inactive, shoots Wing down. He ditches at sea. Relena sees the light, thinks it may be a meteor, and wishes for a world without war. The butler Pagan wants her to go back; she stays to wait for her father.

## 5. ヒイロとリリーナ

Walkthrough heading: ヒィロとリリーナ. Real route.

- On the beach Relena finds Heero. When he realizes she saw his face, he runs. She introduces herself. Pagan takes her to her father, the diplomat Dornian.
- At Jaburo the staff ask why a space-security unit came to Earth. Bright cites the Poseidal invasion and the communications blackout. They say command now sits with Jamitov, the Staff’s communications were knocked out, and Londo Bell should follow Jamitov. They cut the line, claiming eavesdroppers.
- At the Titans HQ, Adelaide, Jamitov says stopping Poseidal is their own business, the Federation is unstable, and there will be no supplies. He hopes the independent unit will perform. They do not mention Daba’s group. Bright decides that would only hurt them.
- They head for the Tesla Reich Institute, which the protagonist’s father founded. The protagonist is uneasy because that father is overprotective and wants them married soon. A California distress call is Poseidal again. Mactomin of the Thirteen, in an Atoll V, is the leader. The local Nemo force runs when Londo Bell arrives.
- Turn 4: Kouji in Mazinger Z, Boss in Boss Borot, and Sayaka in Aphrodite A appear from the institute (the story text says the lower right; the checkpoint says the lower left).
- Afterward Kouji’s team is back, and the war is clearly widening. Poseidal is hitting military sites, including Jaburo. Sayaka fears for her father in Japan.
- At school, classmates gossip about a transfer student, Heero Yuy. He tells Relena he will kill her (お前を殺す). He rewrites his records to cover tuition and checks Londo Bell’s next move. The guide says his reason for hunting them is still unclear.

## 6. 使徒、出現

Walkthrough heading: 使徒、出现. Real route. The chart’s super parallel is 使徒、襲来, not narrated here.

- Relena now treats Heero’s threat as her own secret.
- Next destination: the Minamihara Institute, because they need Combattler V. Kouji wonders about Hyoma’s team.
- Poseidal’s second wave: Gablae, Lessie (another of the Thirteen), and Hasha. Gablae wants a rematch. Lessie identifies the “traitor” as Daba Myroad.
- Same map as California. If either Gablae or Lessie falls, Poseidal retreats. Then the 3rd Angel Sachiel appears from the sea. Attacking it triggers dialogue. Shu in the Granzon appears at the lower left, tells them not to fight it, and the Angel withdraws. The guide says it is effectively impossible to destroy at this point because of the AT Field.
- Turn 1 enemy phase is Heero’s event, told as a cutaway: Relena has wandered near the battle. Heero moves to destroy Londo Bell, then protects her from an enemy and is shocked at himself. She recognizes the pilot. He leaves. She connects him with the “meteor.”
- After the Angel flees, Shu says he only knows it is called an Angel, something between living thing and machine, and that the barrier is an AT Field. He tells them to learn more from NERV in Japan. Amuro does not trust him because Shu betrayed them before. Shu says he has no reason to oppose them now. Bright chooses to believe him. Shu’s memory is not fully back; the guide comments that revival is not easy. He leaves.
- Bright explains NERV: a UN special agency now under the Federation, secretive, HQ in Hakone in the Tokyo-3 under construction, spending heavily on a secret new weapon. Ryoma recalls how strict Dr. Saotome’s technical exchanges with them were.
- On the Poseidal side, pursuit of Londo Bell is delegated to Gablae. Lessie decides that if the outside fails, she will destroy them from inside, and infiltrates.
- Relena, left behind, thinks Heero should come and kill her.

## 7. エヴァンゲリオン始動

Walkthrough heading: エヴァンゲリオン、始動. Marked shared. The guide says real and super differ only in which machines are available.

- Kyao and Mora are stretched as mechanics. Supplies are nearly gone, including Saotome’s reserves. They hope for Banjo’s Halan group, which has not called.
- They reach Japan. At NERV, an Angel has passed the second defense line. Ordinary attacks fail. Commander Gendo Ikari is unconcerned.
- The General Staff gives him operational command but tells him to preserve fighting strength. The author reads this handover as a prearranged trap. That is commentary, not a line from Gendo.
- Misato brings Gendo’s son Shinji. It is not an evacuation. Ritsuko asks if the child is a suitable pilot (適格者). Shinji is shown Evangelion Unit-01, a man-made humanoid weapon. Gendo’s greeting becomes an order to launch. Unit-00 is frozen. Rei Ayanami is badly hurt but is told to get up anyway. Unit-01 shows signs of starting. Shinji boards. His sync rate is far beyond a first contact.
- If Unit-01’s HP hits 0, it goes berserk, attacks, and Sachiel retreats. The guide says this loss is mandatory.
- After the berserk event, Gablae’s Poseidal force appears from the lower left and the right, and Londo Bell from the upper left. Poseidal retreats if Gablae is shot down or if six or fewer enemies remain.
- Gendo expected Londo Bell. He talks, then refuses: fighting Angels is NERV’s job. They fight Poseidal while NERV watches.
- Shinji survives, unconscious for a time. Bright’s offer of help is refused. Gendo will not share Angel intelligence.
- Leaving NERV, they take in an injured civilian. She is Lessie. She is surprised the infiltration was so easy.
- At school, Relena finds Heero has already transferred out again.

Amu identifies her as one of the Thirteen. Daba tells her the war is pure invasion and asks her to stop serving Poseidal. Bright believes him but thinks he is not fully one of them. Amuro, as a Newtype, feels Daba is hiding something. The guide does not say what that secret is.

Kouji’s trio reinforces on turn 3. Shinobu names the unit: Earth Federation Far East Pacific 12th Division, Beast Machine Corps. They were already slated to join.

He asks everyone where Kyouji is. Kouji’s group does not know. Brocken seems to know and refuses to say. Domon treats that as a challenge and attacks the DC force. He does not attack Londo Bell. ## 10. 裏切り

Marked shared.

- The old man is Dr. J, a scientist. He sent Heero to Earth. Heero was trained from childhood to fight and kill, which Dr. J calls a path to peace. Relena rejects that. Dr. J says Londo Bell is only the tail of the organization. The root is the Federation’s corrupt system, now extreme in the Titans. He says Londo Bell, though still Federation and unable to disobey orders, is not a threat to the colonies as a security force. Heero’s attack was an unforeseen situation. He spoke because her eyes match Heero’s. Relena is sent back to Earth.
- Henken, with Jamitov’s permission, delivers the old battleship Argama. He says it should have been the Ra Cailum. He also brings replacement machines, pilots, and a person.
- That person is Lieutenant Colonel Shapiro Keats, the Beast Corps commander, who asks to join as operations staff. Shinobu is silently against it. Shapiro notices the report never justified the claim that the unknown machine is not a Heavy Metal. Bright admits the Pentagona cooperators and that this was kept from HQ. Shapiro approves: HQ cannot judge. Sara later says Shapiro once submitted a plan against an alien invasion and was ignored. In the data room he is struck by technology beyond Earth’s and starts to change.
- The new pilot Birgit talks with Judau about Seabook and the F91. Amuro receives the Re-GZ, plus some Jegans.
- Unidentified machines appear: not Poseidal, and some types never seen. Zeb and an older commander (Seti is with him; the draggy speaker matches Seti’s later manner) hail Londo Bell. They settle on the name Guest and declare war as allies of Pentagona. Bright remembers Guest asked for relations three years ago, and the official record was erased. The fight starts.
- If either Zeb or Seti is shot down, or on turn 8, the first Guest wave retreats. Poseidal then reinforces from a lower island, including Gablae and Lessie. Daba can persuade Lessie. One success and she becomes an ally immediately, still in her machine.

- Bright and Amuro have no access to Guest files and can only ask Cowen, who is now outside the center. The protagonist, overhearing, remembers a top-secret Tesla Reich file about “Guest” from singularity research. They do not know if it is the same Guest. Bright is shocked a civilian lab had it. Tesla Reich is backed by Banjo’s Halan group, so they will try to call Banjo again.
- Shapiro tells Sara he has discarded Earth. People here rejected his ideas, so he will go to space and make the universe acknowledge him. He asks if Guest put this in his head and says that alone would not satisfy him. He invites her. She agrees. The guide reads this as her feelings for him. They steal two fast transports.
- Shinobu sorties alone. Sara says she is following Shapiro’s decision. Shinobu disables her machine. Shapiro escapes without waiting. Sara (結城少尉) is in the infirmary with a minor fall injury. Londo Bell has lost him.

## 14A. ゴラオン救出

Marked shared. Route A.

- Bright picks Goraon first because South America is close to Jaburo.
- Poseidal has Goraon surrounded. Elena does not want to fight surface people and does not know these are invading aliens. She tries to talk. Mactomin’s force will not answer. She feels a malicious aura and only then orders battle.
- Turn 2: the main force from above. Turn 10 enemy phase: Zeb’s Ryg-Geios and Guest troops on the right edge. Zeb retreats on the enemy phase of turn 11.
