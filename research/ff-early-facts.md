# SRW F Final — early / mid scenario facts (through ソラと大地のケモノ)

Source: SDDD walkthrough on srworld.net, pages `srwff01.htm`–`srwff27.htm`, plus split pages `srwff11-1.htm` (Super 絆) and `srwff21-1.htm` (ビヨン・ザ・トッド). Flag mechanics that a stage page only hints at are marked **[opt]** and come from the same author’s `intro/12-OptionalEvents.htm`, not from the stage prose. Combat tactics, money, and upgrade talk are omitted. Author asides that the page itself marks as imagination are not treated as plot.

## Numbering

- `srwff01` says the guide’s stage numbers assume the F-prologue hidden stage 敵包圍網を突破せよ was cleared. Skip that stage, or start F Final directly, and every count is **one lower**.
- Split titles are printed with two numbers on the same page: Real is one higher than Super (`うずまく悪意` 35/34, then 36/35). From ラビアンローズ the shared pages use one “共通” number that continues the higher (Real / hidden-stage) count. `srwff11-1` titles Super 絆 as **第44話** while Real ティターンズの追撃 is **第45話**, and the next shared page is already **第46話**. **[opt]** calls both “第45話（未打隐藏关则是44話）” with different titles. Treat the printed title as the label; do not invent a missing stage between 44 and 46.
- Below, the number in the heading is the number printed on that page.

## Real 第35話 うずまく悪意（前） / Super 第34話 フロンティア4の危機（前）

`srwff01.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Continues from F. Haman’s DC has just been driven off. Londo Bell meets Relena of the Sanc Kingdom. Quattro and Blex narrate AEUG: Blex, a colony-born Federation officer, gathered people who opposed Earth’s treatment of the colonies and wanted a negotiated peace; radicals then armed themselves and pushed independence, backed by DC, which is using the Zabi name and colony strength to rule the Earth Sphere. DC’s base is asteroid Axis, bought from the Federation by a civilian firm that is a DC front. Misato and the others are shaken by how far the Federation has rotted. Plan: hit Axis early to stop a Federation–colony war. Quattro agrees to settle things with Haman because he holds himself responsible. Blex asks Gilliam to watch Sanc; Gilliam will return to AEUG after resupply.
- **Events.** En route, a distress call from Side 4 Frontier 4, under DC attack. Seabook and Cecily are already holding the exterior against Cima (Gerbera Tetra). DC commits hidden reinforcements. Turn 3, the Londo Bell main force arrives. Cima is beaten. The colony interior still has to be cleared (next stage).
- **Joins / flags.** Seabook (F91) and Cecily (Vigna Ghina) are the first F Final recruits, already on the field and heavily damaged. No persuade, death, or route flag.

## Real 第36話 うずまく悪意（後） / Super 第35話 フロンティア4の危機（後）

`srwff02.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Fleet enters the colony port. DC is already deployed. (Super’s opening lines are only the sortie order; the page says so.)
- **Events.** Enemy reinforcements on enemy phase of turn 2: Gaia, Ortega, Mash, and more. After the clear, all five Gundam boys are together for the first time and recognize each other’s machines. Quatre learns Heero’s mission is to destroy Londo Bell, “the enemy of the colonies,” and tries to talk him down. Heero: he will decide his own justice. Quatre: the colonies themselves chose war, so their original mission is over and this fight is the last. Then Titans arrive under Lady Une. She threatens a nuclear missile strike on the satellite unless the pilots surrender and hand over the Gundams. Zechs and Noin try to stop her in Treize’s name; she says she is carrying out Treize’s will and that colonies are fragile little worlds of pitiful people. She orders the launch. Dr. J (the old man who had spoken alone with Relena; Judau recognizes him from the junk shop) cuts in: the colonies are not gathering for war; he is challenging Titans personally, and a nuclear attack is pointless, so he surrenders. Une orders the five suits disarmed. Heero steps out; Bright realizes this is the Heero Yuy Relena spoke of. Heero: they may surrender, but they will not give up the Gundams. Wing self-destructs. Trowa takes the wounded Heero away; Londo Bell does not see that. Une blames Bright for letting them escape and threatens a court-martial. A message from Treize stops her. Treize accepts her unauthorized nuclear threat, tells her the real enemy is the Guest–Poseidal alliance, notes DC is spreading faster than expected, and gives her a special task: the rose is beautiful but has thorns; hard suppression only feeds the weak side’s hostility, which is how DC won the colonists’ trust, and not all of them support DC. Bright, Amuro, and Seabook’s pair conclude DC engineered the unusually unified independence unrest, even dragging in Frontier 4, which had meant to wait and see. Beating Haman would make DC a mob, but Bright admits that without Federation reform another Haman would appear. They decide to talk to Treize. Treize already disliked Jamitov’s methods and, to stop a full war, has named Lady Une goodwill ambassador to the colonies. When Bright says defeating Haman would free the colonies from a useless war, Treize’s expression turns odd. Kouji calls him secretive; Masaki agrees and is angry at the arrogance, which Ryoma links to Shu. Relena, told of the self-destruct, insists Heero is alive.
- **Flags.** Heero’s self-destruct is story, not a choice. No join. Lady Une’s ambassadorship and Treize’s odd reaction are setup, not a player flag.

## 共通 第37話 ラビアンローズ

`srwff03.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Why DC hit Frontier 4 is still unclear. Quattro is asked about the “collaborators” DC said were there, and says he will introduce someone they know. Gilliam has already arranged a stop at the supply ship La Vie en Rose, on the straight line from Frontier 4 to Axis. Bright is unusually shaken. The caller is Emma: she started preparing supplies as soon as she knew he was at Frontier 4. El tells Nerv that Emma likes the captain; Mirai is already his wife and they have a child. Bright denies anything between them. On arrival Emma embraces him. Milly and Nina, former Anaheim juniors of Emma, work there too. ZZ is tuned (Judau). Nina teases Kou about GP-03 Dendrobium, then says it suits him. The new mothership Nahel Argama is due but late. DC catches up. The lead suit is GP-02, piloted by Gato, an ally in the Inspector incident (3rd) and the La Gias incident (EX). Kou demands why. Gato: the Federation is rotten from the root, and Jamitov’s people cannot change the world; he is helping DC because Dozle entrusted Mineva to him. “Questions are useless.”
- **Events.** Turn 12, or earlier if the enemy is wiped, Lune (Valsione R) arrives on the right. If she arrives mid-battle she recognizes Gato, asks why he returned to DC, and tells him to take Mineva and leave. She argues that she is Bian’s daughter, founder of DC, and is fighting DC, so a Zabi name means nothing. Gato refuses to make a fool of Dozle. If she arrives only after the wipe, the Gato argument does not play; she and Masaki bicker until Kouji’s group stops them. Either way: she had Ibn tune the machine, then followed Masaki to Earth. Masaki complains; she says he came down first. Kuro asks her for a spare sync module; Psy Flash is repaired (it had broken because Masaki was careless). Second supply drop: more old MS, including a G-Cannon for Birgit and a Core Booster rebuilt with new parts. Bernie frets over his old Zaku; Chris’s offer to trade for the NT-1 turns awkward. The Core Booster pilot is Sleggar, a comrade from the Inspector war, which makes Bright awkward in front of Milly because of Mirai. Nina is hurt by Gato’s defection; Kou tells her not to mind. Someone (unnamed in the prose) cannot forget a chill — the page treats it as a warning of a larger crisis.
- **Joins.** Lune joins. No death. No route flag. After the stage the player chooses which machine gets the spare Buster Launcher (L-Gaim or Nouvelle Desert).

## 共通 第38話 アクシズの攻防

`srwff04.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Emma sees Bright off. Aboard, El, Beecha, and Judau eavesdrop on Bright missing Emma; he catches them. Milly has already spread the “見損ない” rumor to the protagonist. Judau advises Bright to contact his wife, and says he and the others can clear the misunderstanding except with Ibuki, who is fastidious. Bright explains himself to Milly. Axis planning starts late. AEUG data: the N Field (map north) is the weak point. Misato’s plan: a small force feints at the S Field while the main force hits the N Field. Feint team: Amuro (leader), the four Beast Machine pilots, Shingo’s trio, Shou and Cham, Masaki, and both protagonists. Mashymre leads the Axis response. Haman is surprised they arrived so fast.
- **Events.** Turn 4, both main forces appear. Quattro can keep persuading Haman (see flags). She rejects him: he revealed Newtypes but will not rule by them; she calls him a Newtype who can do nothing. Enemy phase of turn 7, Sandrock (Quatre) enters on the left, lost after the self-destruct. Elle senses no hostility; Camille agrees the earlier oppressive feeling is gone. Amuro or Camille: “We are not enemies.” Quatre asks to join Londo Bell; the Newtype sense confirms he is sincere. He joins. Haman feels a pressure unlike Londo Bell. Mineva calls for her, frightened. Haman withdraws (under 90% HP, or enemy phase of turn 8). Axis still has forces inside, so a second battle is expected. Amuro, Camille, Elle, and Gilliam all feel a heavy premonition. Ritsuko scoffs at the non-scientific warning until Masaki points out that Kuro and Shiro talk; she drops it. Bright delays the all-out assault. If Quatre joined: he is startled that the crew are his age, calls Gundam a hero who frees people from tyranny, and Asuka half-trusts him because he resembles the old Shinji. Misato appears because “a new pretty boy joined.” On Earth, Jamitov questions Treize for naming Lady Une ambassador without permission, then lets it pass. Bask is annoyed. Jamitov will not wait for Treize’s colonial peace: the negotiation with Byston Well goes to Scirocco, not Bask. Scirocco appears in the Titans command room — Bask thought he had died in the previous war and objects that he caused the DC rebellion. Jamitov is calm (ability, and a blood oath). Scirocco names the other party: Drake Luft, supreme commander of the Byston Well army. The page treats Scirocco’s return as the likely source of the oppressive feeling.
- **Joins.** Quatre, if persuaded by Amuro or Camille on turn 7.
- **Flags.** Haman persuade count as in the index. Quatre recruit unlocks the later Wing thread; the page only says his joining avoids a dark future. **[opt]** not recruiting him is a Bad Ending condition and drops Duo/Trowa/Heero.

## 共通 第39話 ザビ家の亡霊

`srwff05.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Londo Bell holds outside Axis. Inside, Mineva asks if they are about to be attacked. Haman stays with her. Mineva is terrified of something approaching and begs Haman not to leave. Mashymre arrives with news he will not say in front of Mineva. Haman is shaken and goes out to “receive” whoever is coming. Outside, a large ship is detected on a Minovsky interference wave. Amuro, Quattro, and Gilliam feel a familiar chill. Axis then attacks first, so Bright sorties everyone.
- **Events.** Fight is only about three and a half turns: reinforcements at the end of allied phase 4, or on a wipe, and the map forces over immediately. Haman’s flagship appears. She is oddly calm when told her stronghold will be destroyed. Three ships follow: Dolos-class carriers. Gihren, then Dozle and Kycilia, are alive. Quattro is hit hardest. Gihren tells them to treat the Zabis as ghosts. The three ships each have battalion-level firepower; Bright orders a retreat to Londenion. Amuro will not rule out survival in the earlier chaos, but there has been no sign of them for months, and the pressure just now felt stronger than before. They cannot finish it here. On Axis, Gihren thanks Haman for keeping DC in order and says they do not clearly remember what happened; they simply found themselves alive. Dozle wants to see Mineva. Haman has Mashymre watch the three: DNA matches, but something is subtly different from the previous three.
- **Joins / deaths / flags.** None. The page’s line about Mineva crying behind Haman is marked as the author’s invention; do not use it. Domon’s level is mentioned only as a later Master Asia condition, with no number on this page.

## 共通 第40話 ガラスの王国

`srwff06.htm`

- **Pre-battle.** Dozle reunites with Mineva. She recognizes him, then screams that he is not her father. Haman’s suspicion deepens. Dozle is only hurt and leaves her with Haman. Gihren and Kycilia announce a new plan: drop the all-colony aid / colonial federation and make Side 3 the base of an independent state. They do not want extra population; “foolish” people would only obstruct an ideal state. Haman is warned not to waste her achievements. At Londenion, Londo Bell is still reeling when Blex reports Titans attacking the Sanc Kingdom. The fleet turns around.
- **Events.** Jamaican is crushing Sanc’s tiny garrison. Inside, Blex’s group is helpless. Relena asks them to open a channel. She asks them to stop. Jamaican’s terms: Sanc gives up autonomy and submits to Titans, and Relena comes to Earth. She will not give up independence but agrees to go herself. Londo Bell arrives before that. Bright demands a reason. Jamaican, under Jamitov’s special order, refuses and threatens a court-martial if they interfere. Bright: their duty is to protect civilians; an independent unit does not watch civilians die. They sortie against their nominal superiors. Three Ball NPCs are present at the start. Titans withdraw when fewer than 6 enemy units remain, or if Jamaican’s Alexandria is shot down. A surviving Ball at clear is obtained.
- **Departures.** Afterward, Zechs asks to leave Londo Bell and stay in Sanc, and tells Bright to report him dead to Treize. Bright agrees. Gilliam also leaves; he is back at the AEUG post and was only traveling with them. Noin stays with Zechs for Relena. Treize is not angry about the Sanc fight, says he will explain it to Jamitov, and does not blame Bright for the “deaths.” He calls Zechs **Milliardo** and says he will wait. The page asks the reader to connect that name to Zechs; it does not confirm the identity in dialogue.
- **Flags.** Ball only. No W / Todd / Four flag.

A second launcher can go on L-Gaim or Nouvelle Desert; the player chooses. The page says Amandaara’s real nature is worse than a profiteer, but does not reveal it here.
