# SRW F — SUPER-route plot facts

Source: srworld.net SUPER walkthrough, stages 1–34  
(http://www.srworld.net/gonglue/srw/srwf/super/1-5.htm through 31-34.htm).

Titles below are the headers printed on those pages (GBK). Tactics, stat tables, and upgrade advice are omitted. Stages whose header includes 共通 are treated as shared with the Real route; only a Super-specific delta, a flag, or a story beat the title does not carry is kept.

The pages never use the labels 迂回しない / 迂回する.

## Branch rules

After 第13话 浮上, news footage shows ゴラオン in the Amazon and グラン=ガラン near Kiev. The walkthrough’s rule, in its own words: 大家要先去哪边呢？……这就是分支了，S现在把选择先救ゴラオン的路线为A，先救グラン=ガラン的路线为B。

- Route A continues as 14A ゴラオン救出 → 15A 標的はロンド=ベル → 16A デビルガンダム再び (the written グラン=ガラン rescue is the opening of 16A).
- Route B is not given its own グラン=ガラン stage text. The pages only label 15B ロンド=ベルへの刺客 and 16B ゴラオンの危機. 15B is the same story and enemy layout as 15A, except the player already has ビルバィン. 16B is the same story as 14A, with a different forced first sortie (see below).

Lhasa detour, printed only as a route-A scene, on the way to the photon-power lab: 如果选“迂回前进”就直接进入光子力研究所的剧情，如果选“一直前进”的话便进入额外的一话“敵包圍網を突破せよ”。

The same paragraph says this choice appears only if route A was already chosen. The pages do not describe a matching choice on route B.

光子力研究所 and ジェットスクランダー are not an automatic Real/Super split. Both sit on 共通 stages. 迂回前进 skips straight into the photon-lab plot; 直行 inserts 第17话 敵包圍網を突破せよ and the lab plot still follows. 第18话 ジェットスクランダー危機一髮！ is marked 共通, with the note that if the extra map was not played the stage number drops by 1. Super vs Real on that map is only reinforcement position, plus one enemy that exists only after the extra map (see stage 18).

There is no separate stage titled グラン・ガラン救出 or グラン=ガラン救出 on these pages.

## SUPER-only opening (headers are not 共通)

### 第1话 對決！？マジンガーZ VSゲッターロボG

- Pre-battle: Sunday at the photon lab. さやか wakes 甲儿; the Tesla Reich graduate student (the protagonist) is already waiting. 早乙女博士 calls 弓教授: Baron Ashura stole Getter Robo G while Ryoma’s team was testing the old Getter.
- Events: Kouji launches Mazinger Z before the order. The protagonist sorties the Gespenst to help. Ashura cannot handle G. Boss Borot arrives first; Brocken sends mechanical beasts. The walkthrough frames recovery as destroying the stolen G. Turn 3, Ryoma’s trio arrive from the bottom of the map in the original Getter-1. On the enemy phase after Getter G (Ashura) is shot down, Brocken retreats.
- After: G is badly damaged. Repair is said to take half a year. The protagonist’s father, director of Tesla Reich and an old acquaintance of Saotome, is researching Getter rays; the protagonist offers to have Tesla Reich repair G. The team is back on the old Getter.
- Joins: protagonist with the Gespenst, as the visitor who sorties. Ryoma’s team returns mid-battle in the old machine.
- Leaves: none.
- Flags: none stated.
- Uncertainty: “half a year” is the characters’ estimate, not a later on-screen timer.

### 第2话 空からの衝擊

- Pre-battle: the father agrees to the repair. Ryoma, Kouji, and the others ship G toward the US Tesla Reich HQ. Kouji interned there recently. A meteor that should have hit the sea changes course and strikes nearby. The crater is small for the impact.
- Events: Dr. Hell is already at the crater and tells them to leave it; he does not open by wanting a fight. The Federation Pacific 12th Division beast-machine squad (Shinobu plus three) arrives, orders the civilians out, and nearly clashes with Kouji until Sara and Sayaka stop it; they then fight together. Jack’s Texas Mack reinforces at the start. Turn 2, the beast squad appears upper-middle. At the end of turn 2, Devil Gundam appears and immediately leaves. Ghoul (Dr. Hell) at half HP: the whole enemy force retreats.
- Mid-battle story: a giant robot flies out of the crater. Even DC says it does not match their report. It flees northwest. Ryoma feels he has seen that kind of machine before. They do not catch it. Dr. Hell’s plan fails.
- After: Shapiro Keats, the squad’s superior, ignores the DC fight and the civilians’ help. He confines Shinobu for disobeying orders and insulting a superior, and sends Sara on after the machine. He detains the protagonist, Kouji, and the others as civilians who heard a military secret, in handcuffs. A sudden order reverses him and he releases them. Sayaka had not reached Prof. Yumi, so the source of the order is unknown. Sara does not catch the machine.
- Joins / leaves: Texas Mack and the four beast-machine pilots are battle reinforcements only. The upgrade note later treats Texas Mack as a unit that remains available; the story does not narrate a formal enlistment. The beast squad does not join. The detainees are released.
- Flags: none.
- Uncertainty: who gave Shapiro the release order.

### 第3话 特訓！大雪山おろし

- Pre-battle: they identify the fugitive as looking like Dr. Kasshu’s Ultimate Gundam (Saotome’s friend; built to regenerate Earth’s environment; it can self-evolve), though Hayato thinks the shape is not quite the same. Prof. Yumi says Cowen, not Yumi, got them released. Space DC is busy and Londo Bell with it. Yumi will look into Kasshu, who has been out of contact. With G gone, Benkei must learn Daisekizan Oroshi. Hayato teaches from what the late Musashi told him. The target is a Megasaurus borrowed from Tesla Reich. Kouji’s baseball image plus a full shout lets Benkei land it. Ashura’s force interrupts. A boy, Domon, cuts both sides’ communications and asks if they have seen a strange person. Ashura refuses. Domon takes that as a challenge and summons a Gundam.
- Events: Shining Gundam starts as an allied third party above the player force. No further reinforcements.
- After: Domon’s partner is Rain, a former assistant of Dr. Kasshu. The battle record lets her confirm the machine is Devil Gundam, not the Ultimate Gundam they knew. Domon is Kasshu’s second son; he left home long ago, so Ryoma’s group never met him when they knew the doctor. On the day Ultimate Gundam was finished, elder son Kyouji stole it and fled toward Earth; escaping pursuit, he shot their mother Mikino. Kasshu was sentenced to cold sleep for responsibility. Domon tries to leave. Ryoma and Kouji offer to chase Devil Gundam with him because DC seems involved. Rain accepts; she has to push Domon into thanking them.
- Joins: Domon (Shining Gundam) and Rain. The upgrade note says Shining Gundam joins at this stage, and that Real does not get it this early.
- Leaves: none.
- Flags: none.
- Uncertainty: the page does not say whether Rain pilots in this battle.

### 第4话 ポセィダルの袭击

Printed title uses 袭击, not 襲撃.

- Pre-battle: in orbit, Daba, Am, and Kyao are about to enter the atmosphere when a Poseidal force attacks. Another unit is already fighting that force. The ship’s entry countdown will not wait, so they enter instead of sortieing, though Daba does not want to abandon the other unit. Nei (Poseidal Thirteen) drops the chase: someone else’s unit is already on Earth, and she is waiting for the declaration of war to be published. Lilina sees two meteor-like objects. Tesla Reich’s Devil Gundam search is still empty, so the group decides to follow DC and heads for Japan, where Dr. Hell’s base is. California is then hit by an unknown force the regular army cannot handle.
- Events: the enemies are not MS, so not DC. A dying Federation soldier says they have something that ignores beam weapons. Daba’s trio has landed in the same area. Daba recognizes Gabre and calls the attack an invasion. Gabre cites Poseidal’s declaration of war: this planet imprisoned and killed their ambassador, so the war is retaliation. Kouji’s group cannot understand the language and thinks of the Inspector from the third war. Domon was in training during that war and barely knew it happened. Daba still judges Poseidal’s stated cause as cover for an invasion and offers to fight with Kouji. Kouji accepts. At battle start, Daba (L-Gaim), Kyao, and Am (Dizard) become allies. Turn 2, Henken (Troy Horse), Roux (Jegan), and Katz (G-Defenser) reinforce from the lower left.
- After: Henken reacts sharply to the name Pentagona. The declaration of war is real and Earth is in confusion. Daba says Poseidal is a dictator and the cause is a pretext. He guesses a backer with advanced jump technology, or the crossing would be impossible. The Inspector name means nothing to Daba. Their mothership Terna was wrecked on entry and has no spare parts. Cham and Lilith look the same; they are Mi-Ferario and Mirarie, not the same person. Daba’s group formally joins. Henken refuses a return to Londo Bell: its independence has been restricted, so it has no grounds to ask civilians for help. Jamitov’s Titans, a Federation security force built partly from ex-DC, is behind that weakening and is meant to take Londo Bell’s place. The party temporarily attaches to Henken’s unit so they can fight Poseidal in the open. Devil Gundam pursuit is shelved. Domon objects; Rain keeps him by calling the fighting training and daring him. Separately, Lilina finds a strange boy on the beach; he asks if she “saw” and runs. Pagan takes her to her diplomat father. The page says this Lilina beat is the same as Real stage 5.
- Joins: Daba, Kyao, Am. Henken’s group is the temporary command they attach to; Roux and Katz are not given a separate enlistment line.
- Leaves: Devil Gundam chase paused, not a character exit.
- Flags: none.
- Uncertainty: the page ties only the beach scene, not the whole stage, to Real stage 5. It does not name Nei’s “other unit” on Earth.

### 第5话 谜の刺客！敵はガンダム！？

- Pre-battle: at school, classmates talk about transfer student Heero Yuy. Lilina recognizes the boy from the beach. He tells her he will kill her, then alters computer-room records to fake his fees and looks up Londo Bell’s movements. Poseidal’s attack has thrown even Jaburo into chaos. Space Londo Bell cannot reach the general staff, contacts Jamitov directly, and Jamitov restores Londo Bell’s independence. Bright calls Henken. Kouji barges in and settles adding the new members. Poseidal attacks again.
- Events: turn 2, Bright’s force arrives lower left; the player freely sorties 6 units. On the turn-2 enemy phase, Heero’s event. If either Gabre or Recci is defeated, that wave all retreats. After the wave is wiped or retreats, Gundam Heavyarms (Trowa) appears on the right as a third party. Unlike Real, shooting down the two bosses separately yields a mobility part from one and an HP part from the other; with no map weapon the player can only secure one.
- Mid-battle: Lilina is in the area and under attack. Heero in Wing confirms Londo Bell and moves to destroy it, then protects Lilina instead and does not understand why. She recognizes the pilot. He leaves. She connects the earlier meteor to Heero. After Poseidal withdraws, Trowa’s Gundam (battle record 001) snipes Londo Bell and is shot down. The group treats it as a new enemy besides Poseidal.
- After: reunited with Bright, Kouji introduces the protagonist (Tesla Reich), Daba’s group from Pentagona, and Domon, whose real goal is different but who is still traveling with them. Rain has to push Domon to greet anyone. Londo Bell’s roster is still thin; Chris calls a Zaku an antique, Bernie objects, and Judau’s friends agree in part because recovered Zakus sell. Henken moves the supplies he has onto their side and leaves to fight for more resupply. Lilina is left thinking about Heero.
- Joins: Londo Bell main force under Bright, including Amuro and Judau as the pilots the checkpoint names.
- Leaves: Henken separates to seek supplies. Trowa does not join; he is fought as a third party.
- Flags: Gabre vs Recci is an item choice, not a story flag.
- Uncertainty: why Heero is hunting Londo Bell is not answered here.

### 第6话 使徒、襲來

- Pre-battle: Londo Bell is short of mechanics, so Amuro is servicing machines. Rain offers to help and says she maintains Shining Gundam. She corrects the type to Mobile Fighter, built for competition, with a mobile-trace system, and says the pilot summons it by voice through a homing device. Amuro compares that to Banjou’s Daitarn 3. Next destination is Nanbara Institute in Japan, for Combattler V; Kouji thinks of Hyoma, and of asking the Banjou group for supplies. At sea they meet Poseidal. The lead HM is Auge, piloted by Nei, one of the Poseidal Thirteen who had been chasing Daba. She is not careless about the force that beat Gabre.
- Events: turn 7 enemy phase, a Poseidal reinforcement (including Gabre in Ashlatemple and Recci) appears right of where Nei started. After the enemy is wiped, the third angel Sachiel appears in the central sea as a third party. Attacking the angel triggers dialogue; then Shu in the Granzon appears at the lower right, an event plays, and the angel retreats. The page says the angel’s method and the event’s details are the same as Real stage 6, and does not print them. It also says the post-battle plot is completely the same as the Real event and does not retell it.
- Joins / leaves: none narrated on this page.
- Flags: none beyond the Shu/angel event being declared identical to Real.
- Uncertainty: dialogue text and the aftermath are explicitly off this page. The header is not marked 共通, but the author says the story result matches Real.

## Detour and the photon-lab / Jet Scrander stage

### 第17话 （额外路线）敵包圍網を突破せよ

Only if route A was chosen and the player picks 直行. Marked 共通 aside from that gate.

- Pre-battle: Earth Federation bases are essentially all in Guest–Poseidal hands. Titans bases in space are intact; colonies are not. Bright explains two Federation plans: a long war that wins on geography, which the brass prefer in order to save themselves, or a short strike at the enemy’s vitals. Londo Bell uses its independence to fight the short war for the civilians’ sake. Shinji is still unsure about piloting Eva. Then the route-A choice: the road to the photon lab passes Lhasa, now an enemy base. Detouring avoids a head-on fight; going straight weakens the enemy. Options are 「迂回前进」 and 「直行」.
- If 直行: Bright chooses to go through. Lhasa airspace reacts at once. Victory is either a wipe or every unit exiting the east edge. Turn 5 enemy phase, Nei’s reinforcement appears at the center, near the player’s start.
- After: they withdraw. The fight is said to have weakened the enemy somewhat. Kouji remembers the second war’s Lhasa escape with Four’s help. Camille says Four is not in the war and does not want her dragged back in. Kouji argues Londo Bell is currently the safest place. Camille agrees to contact her again. They continue to the photon lab.
- Joins / leaves: Four is not shown joining; Camille only decides to call.
- Flags: the menu choice itself. No story flag inside the battle.
- Uncertainty: the page never says route B skips, repeats, or relocates this choice.
